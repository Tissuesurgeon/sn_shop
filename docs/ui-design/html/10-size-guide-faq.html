How to measure

  1. Bust — around the fullest part, tape level.
  2. Waist — the narrowest point, above the navel.
  3. Hips — the fullest part, feet together.

Between sizes? Size up — or choose Made to measure and we'll cut it for you.

Delivery, returns & more

DeliveryPaymentReturns & exchangesMade to measureCare
How fast is delivery in Accra?

Order before 12pm for same-day delivery (GH₵40), or choose next-day (GH₵25). Riders call you before arriving.

Do you deliver outside Accra?

Yes — to all 16 regions by courier or VIP-bus parcel in 2–4 working days for GH₵55. Free on orders over GH₵1,500.

Which payment methods do you accept?

MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money, Visa and Mastercard via Paystack — or order on WhatsApp.

Can I return or exchange?

Unworn items with tags can be exchanged or credited within 7 days. Made-to-measure pieces are final sale, but alterations are free for 14 days.

Can I pick up from the studio?

Yes, pickup in Accra is free. We'll WhatsApp you when your order is ready.
